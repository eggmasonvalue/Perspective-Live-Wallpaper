Two Modes of Perspective

Macro View

Life Calendar

Your life clock on your lock screen. Visualize how much life you've lived versus what you might have left. It's not about fear; it's about clarity and intentionality.

  • Visualizes years lived vs expected (Default 90).
  • Configurable breathing pulse animation paced at 30 FPS.
  • Paired light and dark schemes adapt seamlessly with system theme changes.
  • Midnight boundary scheduler smoothly advances date-dependent renders.
